Our Story

We grow what
we export

Extropica Fresh is a family-run Kenyan grower and exporter of premium fresh herbs. We cultivate our herbs on our own farms in Ruai and Karen, Nairobi — which means single-origin quality we control from the moment a seed goes in the ground to the moment your order ships.

Unlike traders who buy and resell, we own the entire process. Quality decisions are never outsourced — from varietal selection and soil management to harvest timing, grading, packing, and cold-chain dispatch. This direct ownership is our biggest advantage: buyers know exactly where their herbs come from, with full traceability back to our own farm plots.
