Rooted in our own farms. Connected to more growers. Built to meet demand.

Extropica Fresh is a family-run Kenyan grower and exporter of premium fresh herbs. Our own farms in Ruai and Gattaka, Nairobi, form the foundation of our supply.

We have established an ecosystem of contracted farmers to supplement our own production whenever demand requires. Our team coordinates orders, grading, packing, and cold-chain dispatch through one export relationship.

How we work

From our soil to shipment

From our own production and contracted growers to packing and air freight. Follow the journey.

Grow

2

Harvest
