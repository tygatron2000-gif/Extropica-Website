We grow what
we export.

A family-run Kenyan grower-exporter. Single-origin herbs from our own soil in Ruai & Gattaka, Nairobi.

Unlike traders who buy and resell, we own the entire process, seed to shipment.

Extropica Fresh is a Kenyan grower and exporter of premium fresh herbs. We cultivate our herbs on our own farms in Ruai and Gattaka, Nairobi. Single-origin quality we control from the moment a seed goes in the ground to the moment your order ships.

Quality decisions are never outsourced, from varietal selection and soil management to harvest timing, grading, packing, and cold-chain dispatch. This direct ownership is our biggest advantage: buyers know exactly where their herbs come from, with full traceability back to our own farm plots.

How we work

Farm-to-export process

A transparent process we own end to end, from our soil in Nairobi to your global supply chain.

1

Grow

We plant and cultivate herbs on our own farms in Nairobi using sustainable methods and quality seed stock.

2

Harvest

Hand-harvested at peak maturity to maximise flavour, aroma, and shelf-life for international markets.

3

Grade

Every batch is graded and inspected. Only herbs meeting export standards move forward.

4

Pack

Packed in ventilated cartons, clamshells, or retail bunches, tailored to buyer and market.

5

Export

Cold-chain dispatch with full phytosanitary documentation and customs clearance.
